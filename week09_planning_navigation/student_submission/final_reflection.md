## Response

I really enjoyed this lab, especially the automated trials and testing. Being able to run multiple experiments without having to manually control everything made the experience more enjoyable and allowed me to focus on analyzing the results. I also appreciated the visualizations because they made it much easier to understand how the different pathfinding algorithms worked and how the robot navigated around obstacles. This lab positively affected my motivation to continue working with robotics. Seeing the algorithms actually being tested in a simulated environment made the concepts feel more practical and interesting. I particularly liked how hands off the experiments were, since I could focus more on comparing performance, identifying differences, and understanding why certain approaches worked better than others. Another important takeaway was the significance of safety and reliability. A robot finding the shortest path does not necessarily mean it is the safest or most reliable option, especially when navigating around people. This showed me why human considerations are important when designing robotic systems. Overall, I found this lab engaging and relatively relaxed compared to more hands on assignments. I enjoyed its analytical approach, automated testing, and clear visual feedback. It was a positive experience that increased my interest in similar robotics work in the future.
