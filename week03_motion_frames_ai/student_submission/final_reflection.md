# Final reflection

Respond to any or all of these prompts:

1. What did this activity make you think about regarding your own interests in robotics, computing, engineering, or related work?
2. How did this activity affect your motivation to do similar kinds of work in the future?
3. What value do you see in connecting technical or computing work with human, ethical, or societal considerations?
4. What stood out to you about the activity, and why?
5. Is there anything else you would like to share about your experience with the activity?

## Response

I liked that the lab encouraged using AI as part of the development process instead of treating it as something separate from the work. It was useful to generate code, review it, test it, and make changes rather than just accepting the first response. I also enjoyed the trial and error part of predicting the robot’s motion, running it, and comparing the result to what I expected. That made the lab feel more interactive and positively affected my motivation to do similar robotics work in the future.

The activity also reinforced that you always have to consider the context where a robot is operating. Even if the code works correctly, the robot still needs to behave in a way that is safe and understandable around people. This lab did not feel as long as some of the previous work, but it still felt like there was just as much, if not more, technical work involved.
